# Free to Go Further

Campaign website for free public transport for every young person in the UK.

- `index.html` is the whole site. Open it in a browser or serve it with GitHub Pages.
- Settings to change before launch are in the `SETTINGS` block near the end of `index.html`. They cover the contact email, Instagram and LinkedIn addresses, the report link and the Google Form links.
- `assets/` holds the logo, LinkedIn images and social media graphics in portrait and square sizes.
- `tools/uk_transit_gaps_map.py` draws a map of British council areas without a railway station.

Ambassador profiles and case study cards are placeholders. Photos are from Unsplash. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
